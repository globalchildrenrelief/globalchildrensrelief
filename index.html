📖 Our Story

Hello everyone, My name is Linda Lippincott, and I never thought I would one day have to come online and ask strangers for help. Writing this with tears in my eyes is one of the hardest things I have ever had to do, but I truly do not know where else to turn anymore. For a long time now, I have been suffering from severe back pain and constant pain in my eyes that affects me every single day and night. The pain has become so unbearable that most nights I cannot sleep at all. I spend countless hours awake in pain, praying and hoping for relief that never seems to come. Simple daily activities that many people take for granted have become extremely difficult for me. Some days it hurts just to stand, walk, bend, or even rest comfortably. The pain in my back continues to get worse, and the constant discomfort in my eyes has affected my quality of life emotionally and physically. There are nights when I cry quietly because the pain becomes overwhelming and exhausting. I try my best to stay strong and keep smiling, but deep inside I am struggling more than words can explain. Living with pain every day has taken away my peace, my sleep, and so much of my strength. Doctors have recommended treatment and medical care, but the expenses are simply more than I can afford on my own. I never wanted to ask for financial help, but at this point I truly need kindness and support from caring people. Every donation, prayer, kind message, and share means more to me than you could ever imagine. No amount is too small, and every bit of support brings me one step closer to getting the treatment and care I desperately need. From the bottom of my heart, thank you for taking the time to read my story. Thank you for your compassion, your prayers, your kindness, and your support during this difficult time in my life. May God bless each and every one of you ❤️
